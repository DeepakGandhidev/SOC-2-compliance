# What each page targets

One primary query per page, chosen by **buying intent, not search volume**.

## The honest caveat first

There is no keyword tool behind these numbers, because there are no numbers. Nothing here
carries a monthly search volume or a difficulty score, and anything claiming otherwise
without a data source is guessing. These were picked by a different question: *would
somebody typing this have the problem SOC2Starter solves?*

Real volume data arrives free once Google Search Console is verified and the sitemap is
submitted — impressions per query, for this site specifically, which beats any third-party
estimate. **Write the next guides from that data, not from this file.**

### Why these seven

The pattern is deliberate. Somebody searching *"what evidence does a SOC 2 auditor ask
for"* has already decided to get SOC 2, has hit a specific wall, and is looking for the
thing this product produces. Somebody searching *"what is SOC 2"* is a student or a
curious founder two years from buying.

The evidence checklist is the most valuable of the seven, because it is the only one a
competitor cannot honestly copy — it is generated from the product's real 29-requirement
catalogue.

## The 47 question targets

Every FAQ entry is a query in its own right, marked up with `FAQPage` schema so it can be
answered directly in a search result or lifted by an answer engine. This is the half of
the strategy aimed at ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews rather than at ten blue
links, and it is where a brand-new site can realistically win first.

**Cost** — how much does SOC 2 cost for a startup · is SOC 2 a one-time cost · why is Type 2 more expensive than Type 1 · can I get SOC 2 without a compliance platform · does SOC 2 require a penetration test

**Evidence** — how much evidence does a SOC 2 audit need · difference between a policy and evidence · can I create SOC 2 evidence retroactively · most common SOC 2 finding for startups · do I need a penetration test

**Policies** — what policies are required for SOC 2 · can I use SOC 2 policy templates · who has to approve a SOC 2 policy · how often do policies need review · how long should a policy be

**Readiness** — how do I prepare for a SOC 2 audit as a small startup · how long does readiness take · what should a startup do first · do I need a consultant · when should a startup start

**Timeline** — how long does SOC 2 take · can you get SOC 2 in 30 days · how long is the observation period · what delays an audit most · should I start before a customer asks

**Type 1 vs 2** — what is the difference · which should a startup get first · how long is the observation period · does a report expire · what is a bridge letter

**ISO 27001** — what is the difference · which first · does SOC 2 count towards ISO 27001 · is ISO harder · can you do both at once

**Product** (pricing, about, contact) — what does the trial include · what happens when it ends · can I get a refund · does this make us SOC 2 compliant · do you store our evidence files · can we cancel · who builds it · who is it for · what makes it different · how do I start a trial

## What is deliberately not targeted

- **"soc 2 compliance"** and **"what is soc 2"** — enormous volume, near-zero buying
  intent, and owned by AICPA, Vanta and Drata. Chasing them from a new domain is a way to
  produce content nobody reads.
- **"vanta alternative"**, **"drata alternative"** — genuinely valuable and worth writing
  later, but a comparison page from a site with no traffic and no reviews converts badly
  and invites a fight you cannot win yet.
- **Anything about ISO 27001, HIPAA or PCI as a product claim.** The product does SOC 2
  Security only. Ranking for a framework you do not support wastes the click and the trust.
