## Filling them in

- **Always link the canonical domain**, no tracking parameters, no trailing slash — it
  matches the canonical tags already on every page.
- **Use the real screenshots**, not mockups. This audience checks.
- **Category: pick "GRC" or "Security Compliance", never "Security"** generically —
  you want the comparison set to be Vanta and Drata, where the price is the story.
- **Do not claim ISO 27001, HIPAA or PCI support.** The product does SOC 2 Security only.
  Listing frameworks you do not support wastes the click and burns the trust the rest of
  the site works hard to earn.
