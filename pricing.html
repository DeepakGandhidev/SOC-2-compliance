Questions people ask before paying

What happens when the trial ends?

Your workspace becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted — every policy, file and task stays exactly where it is, and stays readable. Subscribe and editing turns back on where you left off.

Can I get a refund?

No. Because there is a full-featured free trial before any payment, subscriptions are non-refundable. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Read the refund policy before you subscribe.

Does this make us SOC 2 compliant?

No, and be wary of anyone who says their product does. SOC 2 is an examination performed by an independent CPA firm. SOC2Starter prepares you for it: it shows what is missing, drafts the policies, organises the evidence and keeps the recurring work on schedule. The percentage it shows is our readiness estimate, not an official score.

Do you store our evidence files?

Yes — that is the product. They live in a private bucket that is not publicly readable, downloads use short-lived signed links, and every access is logged. See the privacy policy for where the data sits and who processes it.

Can we cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your billing portal and the subscription stops renewing. You keep access for the rest of the period you have already paid for.
