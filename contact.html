What to write to us about

Starting a trial

Tell us your company name and how many people are on the team, and we will get you set up. Fourteen days, no card.

Billing and invoices

Payments are handled by our payment provider, who acts as merchant of record. Invoices, VAT numbers and receipts come from them — the receipt email you get after checkout links to your billing portal. If you cannot find it, email us. Please read the refund policy before subscribing: subscriptions are non-refundable.

Security questions and vendor reviews

If you are reviewing us as a vendor, ask. We will tell you where your data lives, which subprocessors touch it and how tenant isolation is enforced. Most of it is already written down in the privacy policy.

Reporting a vulnerability

Email us with the details and steps to reproduce, and please give us a reasonable window to fix it before disclosing publicly. We will confirm receipt and keep you updated. We do not currently run a paid bug bounty, but we will credit you if you want to be credited.

Your data, or deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data yourself, from inside the app, under Settings → Danger zone. For a copy of your data, or any other request under applicable privacy law, email us and we will respond within 30 days.
